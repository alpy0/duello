# Öğlen Düellosu: görsel istekleri

Ortak tarz (her isteğin sonuna eklenir):
> Style: hand-drawn 2D cartoon illustration for a mobile game, clean bold ink outlines, flat cel shading, warm sepia and sun-bleached yellow palette, dusty wild west at high noon. Original design. No text, no logos, no watermark.

Referans olarak başka bir oyunun ekran görüntüsü VERİLMEZ. Tarz sözle tarif edilir.

## 1. Arka plan: kasaba sokağı
Portrait 9:16 background for a first-person duel game. Standing in the middle of a dusty main street of a small wild west town at noon, looking straight down the street. Wooden false-front buildings on both sides (saloon, general store, sheriff office, bank) with porches, hitching posts and water troughs, shrinking toward a vanishing point in the center. Sandy ground with wagon tracks and scattered tumbleweeds. Bright hazy sky with a few puffy clouds. The center of the street is empty so a character can be placed there later. Horizon at about 45% of the height. + ortak tarz

## 2. Tabanca: birinci şahıs
First-person view of a right hand holding a single-action six-shooter revolver, pointing forward and slightly up, seen from behind and below as if the viewer is aiming it. Weathered steel barrel and cylinder, dark walnut grip, leather-brown sleeve cuff at the wrist. Place it at the bottom right. Transparent background, PNG. + ortak tarz

## 3. Rakip karakter 1: "Kızıl Sakal"
Full-body front view of an original wild west outlaw character standing facing the viewer, feet apart, arms relaxed at the sides, right hand near a holstered revolver. Big red beard, dusty grey hat with a bent brim, long tan duster coat, green bandana around the neck, brown boots with spurs. Slightly exaggerated cartoon proportions, readable silhouette from far away. Transparent background, PNG. + ortak tarz

### 3b. Aynı karakter: nişan alırken
The same character as before, same clothes and colors, now aiming a revolver straight at the viewer with the right arm extended, left hand on hip. Full body, front view, transparent background, PNG. + ortak tarz

### 3c. Aynı karakter: vurulmuş, yerde
The same character as before, same clothes and colors, lying on his back on the ground seen from the front, hat fallen next to him, arms spread. Cartoon style, no blood. Transparent background, PNG. + ortak tarz

## 4. Kılıf simgesi (bekleme ekranı)
A single leather gun holster with a revolver inside, hanging from a belt, simple centered icon, front view. Transparent background, PNG. + ortak tarz
